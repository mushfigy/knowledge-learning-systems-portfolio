Why Readiness Cannot Be Verified

BUSINESS CONTEXT

ROOT CAUSES

Fragmented learning content
Materials are not organized by stage or task.

Undefined progression standard
No defined route connects orientation, practice and independent work.

Trainer-dependent delivery
Coverage, feedback and support depend on the individual trainer.

Readiness without standard evidence
Performance cannot be compared against one evidence threshold.

Uncontrolled content lifecycle
Ownership, approval and updating are not controlled.

RELATIONSHIP CAPTION
Uncontrolled maintenance allows fragmented guidance to remain active.

ORGANIZATIONAL DIAGNOSIS
Independent-work readiness cannot be verified consistently.

The organization lacks a controlled connection between learning content, progression, performance evidence and readiness decisions.
